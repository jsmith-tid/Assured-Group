# Prototype #

## Purpose ##

I want to create a prototype of a website. This is just for demonstration to stakeholder purposes so does not need any type of testing. It builds upon an existing website (https://www.assuredgroup.co.nz/) but with a new information architecture and new pages. I want to keep the design similar to what it is now. Obviously, it doesn't need a lot of admin stuff like analytics tags; it just needs to look like the real thing and be navigable but forms do not need to work and neither to emails or telephone numbers. 

The company is a small insurance company doing both B2C and B2B in the area of Nelson / Richmond and wider Tasman areas in New Zealand. Currently, there is no desire to move out of those areas unless the business builds first. In other words, it's locally focused.

You can find out more from our conversation thread "Assured website audit".
